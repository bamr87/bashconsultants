Standards for `pages/_posts/<section>/` (corp, erp, muses, tech). Authoritative sources: `.github/instructions/posts.instructions.md` and `_data/taxonomy.yml`.

**Section voice — corp:** sharp and strategic, for owners and CFOs. Lead with the number or the risk, argue from evidence, close with a decision the reader can make this quarter. Minimal humor, zero whimsy. Common drift to fix: scene-setting ledes that delay the cost, meta framing ("this is the unglamorous version"), and chatty tags ("full stop").

**Section voice — erp:** performed comedy with a real business lesson underneath — scenes, characters, hosts, episode titles, running bits. Keep the comedy; do not sanitize or flatten dialogue. The device is *enacted, never labeled*: no reader-facing text may say "parody", "satire", "sketch", "spoof", "reality show", "musical number", "horror story", or use "imagine a…" framing. In-fiction framing (narrator, host, confessional, episode title, stage direction, "commercial break — the lesson") IS the enactment and stays. Every piece must land one concrete, actionable lesson for whoever signs the checks. Exclamation marks: banned in prose, tolerated but rare inside performed dialogue.

**The ERP Survivor series** is the section's running bit — recurring cast (Linda/Dave/Sarah = Finance "Spreadsheet People"; Bob/Carol/Mike = Operations "Legacy Loyalists"; Jennifer/Tom/Alex = IT "ERP Evangelists"), a Host and a Guest Judge, `> **Commercial break — the lesson:**` blockquotes carrying the business takeaway, and an italic "characters are fictional / next episode" footer. Episodes should wikilink each other for continuity; the "next episode" teaser is a cross-link, not a CTA, so it may sit after the CTA in the footer. The running number gag is **47**.

**Section voice — muses:** reflective and essayistic, lightly witty; longer-form thinking about technology, work, and judgment. Metaphor and structure play are *welcome and load-bearing* — extended conceits (job control as parenting, the triangle as automation geometry, Thrasymachus as procurement), haiku epigraphs, staged scripts with sung verses, and mock press releases all live here. Do not flatten them; the section's rule is that every essay still ends pointed at something the reader can do, and lands a business turn (a "the same bug runs your business" section) before the CTA. Exclamation marks are banned in essay prose but stay inside performed lines (sung verses, in-scene dialogue). Common drift to fix: reader-facing text that *labels* the device (an excerpt describing "a frantic ensemble sings its way…", a heading "The part that isn't a joke", "the song's funniest line"), stray authoring language leaking into prose ("the thing under your brief"), and long unsourced essays that quietly acquire an invented percentage in the last paragraph.

**Section voice — tech:** crisp, actionable how-to for the in-house IT lead. Concrete steps, honest ranges, named watch-outs, plain English before jargon, every acronym expanded on first use. Common drift to fix: essayistic momentum that forgets to leave the reader a step; unsourced market superlatives ("the fastest-moving corner of the market"); vendor/product claims that outrun the linked doc; invented magnitudes ("two orders of magnitude cheaper"); and self-labeled devices ("this isn't a metaphor I'm straining to make fit"). The `bash`/terminal motif is welcome in tech posts where it earns its place.

**Frontmatter key order actually used in corp posts:** `title, description, [excerpt], author, layout, date, lastmod, draft, categories, tags, keywords, preview, [featured]`. Preserve each file's existing order and quoting style.

- `title` ≤ 60 chars — **do not change it**; the preview image filename derives from it (see [[preview-image-system]] in user memory). Propose changes in the report instead.
- `description` 120–155, one sentence, no trailing period. `excerpt` may end with a period; the theme truncates cards at 120 chars, so front-load.
- Bump `lastmod` only, never `date`.
- `categories` flow-style lowercase; primary must equal the subfolder; `ai` is the only sanctioned secondary.
- `tags` 3–8, flow-style kebab-case, topics not audiences. Drop tags that restate the category (e.g. `corporate-strategy` on a `corp` post). Site tag inventory lives in `_data/taxonomy.yml` under `tags.common` plus the tags already in use across posts.
- `keywords` 5–10 real search phrases, flow-style; frequently missing on older posts.

**Links, per post:** exactly one CTA at the very end; at least one *other* internal link in the body (a toolkit doc or sibling post reads as a reference, not a competing CTA); at least one outbound link to a primary source (vendor docs, CISA, NIST, BLS — never a blog).

**Verified wikilink targets** (pipeless, must match `title:` exactly): services — `ERP consulting`, `IT strategy`, `Finance tech`, `AI solutions and intelligent automation`, `Software development`, `Managed IT services`, `Data and BI`, `Cloud architecture`. Toolkit — `Outgrowing QuickBooks: is it time for ERP?`, `ERP implementation architecture`, `Closing your books faster with automation`, `Record-to-report automation and controls`, `The security baseline every small business needs`, `Adopting AI in your business without losing control`, `Building a 12-month IT roadmap`, `Backups and recovery that actually work`. Posts — `ERP Survivor, episode 1: alliance of the spreadsheet people`, `ERP Survivor, episode 2: the great chart of accounts debate`, `An AI acceptable-use policy your team will actually follow`, `When the numbers say it's time to leave QuickBooks`, `Prompts are the new command line`, `From prompts to pipelines: agentic AI in VS Code`, `bashos: the new command-line operating system`, `MCP for the back office`, `The AI audit trail: log prompts like journal entries`; also toolkit `The deterministic-first doctrine`, `Production AI: RAG, agents, guardrails, and evals`, `Running an AI-native consulting practice`, `Building a native Claude context framework`. A colon inside a title is fine in a wikilink; only the pipe breaks it. Root pages (`/tools/`, `/ai-operations/`, `/about/`, `/contact/`) must be markdown links.

**Gate:** `python3 scripts/content_lint.py --warn-only` from the repo root. As of 2026-09-07 the repo baseline is 0 errors / 13 warnings, all in `pages/_about/**` and `pages/_toolkit/constellation-engine.md` — compare against that baseline rather than expecting zero.

**Known citation trap:** the famous "~23 minutes to return to an interrupted task" figure is from Gloria Mark's **2005** "No Task Left Behind?" study, **not** from `ics.uci.edu/~gmark/chi08-mark.pdf` (CHI 2008), which found interrupted work is finished *faster* but with more stress and effort. Attribute the number to her interruption research generally and let the CHI 2008 link carry its own finding.
