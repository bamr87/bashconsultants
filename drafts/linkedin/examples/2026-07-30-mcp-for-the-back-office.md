Your accounting system, your customer records, and your document store each speak a different dialect. That is why "just connect the AI to it" turns into a six-week integration project, and why the demo that impressed everyone never reached production.

The Model Context Protocol is the piece that makes the plug standard — one interface an assistant can speak, instead of a bespoke adapter per system. Useful, not magic: you still decide what it may read, what it may write, and what waits for a person before it commits.

Written for the back office rather than the lab. Worth your time if someone has asked you to point an assistant at the systems that close your books. #MCP #AI #Integration
